# Inexpensive Build Tips

## Robot Parts

#### Cable Management

* Zipties
* Ziptie mount pads (can be 3D printed)
* Hot glue

## 3D Printed Tools

#### Tube Cutting Jig for 1/2" Grid Punched Tube

* This allows you to easily align any saw to cut any 1/2" grid tube to 0.25" or 0.5" spacing from the hole.
* Original Version: [https://www.printables.com/model/373487-jig-for-cutting-10-hole-patterned-box-tube](https://www.printables.com/model/373487-jig-for-cutting-10-hole-patterned-box-tube)&#x20;

#### Gridfinity Storage Organization

* You can use these 3d printed bins for sorting hardware, tools, etc.&#x20;
* [Gridfinity Onshape FeatureScripts](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/f307f8af01083b99b55bbcf9/v/bd0e6d11bef18dbefb06c5fe/e/10837cead0dddf47a9b60a3b) allow you to create grids or bins of any size you need.

## Organization

#### Cafeteria Trays

* We borrowed this idea from FRC#3538
* Cafeteria trays make it very easy to organize fasteners, hardware, and tools while working on the robot. Work can be left over night in a semi-organized manner and doesn't get lost as easily as parts just sitting on a table or on the floor.

#### Hefty HI-RISE Bins

* We have begun switching to these bins for organization and mechanisms, as the larger lids allow more items to fit, and they are inexpensive and robust in our experience.
* They are sold at multiple vendors, including [Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/ip/Hefty-HI-RISE-40-qt-Storage-Bin/287444646) and [Target](https://www.target.com/p/hefty-40qt-clear-plastic-storage-bin-with-gray-hi-rise-stackable-lid/-/A-16644160#lnk=sametab).
